# Machete PS5 Backup Manager

![Machete Hero Image](https://via.placeholder.com/800x400/000000/00ffcc?text=Machete+PS5+Backup+Manager)

> Your PS5 backup library, rebuilt with a cyberpunk edge. 

Machete is a macOS and Windows desktop app designed to seamlessly manage your PS5 backups. Say goodbye to messy directories full of cryptic `.pkg` files and disorganized folders. Machete automatically scans your external drives, reads PPSA IDs, and fetches the official game covers and metadata directly from SerialStation to display your collection beautifully.

## ⚡ Features

* **Cyberpunk UI with Native Feel**: Built with a sleek, neon-infused cyberpunk aesthetic that feels right at home on both macOS (native UI) and Windows.
* **Smart Cross-Region Fetching**: Machete instantly identifies your backups—whether they are single `.pkg` files or extracted folders—and pulls high-quality covers and official names using SerialStation metadata. 
* **100% Private & Secure**: Your collection is your business. All requests for metadata are fully anonymized. No tracking, no telemetry, no nonsense.
* **Full File Management**: Clean up your drive effortlessly. Rename or delete files and folders directly from the Machete interface.
* **Global Access (11 Languages)**: Fully localized in 11 languages out of the box, because your gaming library shouldn't be limited by borders.

## 🚀 Installation

### macOS
1. Download the latest `.dmg` release from the [Releases](https://github.com/your-username/machete/releases) page.
2. Open the file and drag **Machete** to your Applications folder.
3. Launch the app and grant the necessary permissions to read external drives.

### Windows
1. Download the latest `.exe` installer from the [Releases](https://github.com/your-username/machete/releases) page.
2. Run the installer and follow the on-screen instructions.
3. Plug in your drive and start organizing.

## 🕹️ Usage

1. **Connect your drive**: Plug in the external HDD or SSD containing your PS5 backups.
2. **Scan**: Click the "Scan" button in Machete. The app will automatically parse your `.pkg` files and folders for PPSA IDs.
3. **Organize**: Watch as your raw files are transformed into a visual library. You can now safely rename files to standard formats or delete duplicates directly within the app.

## 🌍 Languages Supported
